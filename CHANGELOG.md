# Changelog

All notable changes to Hearth that a user or visitor can see. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Hearth has no numbered releases yet, so everything sits under Unreleased. Features that are built but switched off are listed separately and are not live.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Pick the audience for every photo by name, from plain named groups. A photo you can't see behaves as if it doesn't exist.
- Sign in with a 6-digit code sent by email. No passwords.
- Gallery grouped by month, a search field, and a birthday and important-dates reminder (month and day only).
- Feed with reactions and comments. Each post says who can see it, and the comment field says who will read the reply.
- Share as a postcard: add photos, write a note, then pick who gets it. Nobody is pre-picked.
- Easy view, on by default for grandparents: a word under every icon and bigger text. Text is 17 pt or larger and tap targets are 48 pt everywhere.
- Warm paper look with fruit-inspired colour themes, light and dark, and one text scale at iPhone's standard sizes.
- Quiet by design: no "Seen by 3 of 8" count, so a returned heart shows a photo arrived without counting who didn't look.
- Public demo at chaseholroyd.com/hearth: a made-up family kept in your browser, free stock photos, and "See it as Grandma Jo" to show that each photo reaches only the people it was shared with.
- Add to home screen on the web, and staying signed in on the web.

### Changed
- When a photo list can't load, the screen now says what happened (no signal, or something went wrong), says your photos are safe, and offers "Try again". Saved photos stay on screen with a line above them. Empty lists have a button for what to do next.

### Privacy
- Photos are encrypted on the phone before upload and stored in the US. Hearth's server holds the photo keys today, so this is not end-to-end encryption.
- Location and camera data are removed from photos on the phone.
- The app has no ads, no trackers and no AI training, and runs no AI on photos or metadata.

### Built, switched off
- Text message sign-in, video (with autoplay and a Save data choice), starting a family from the app, and news, event and saying posts.

### Not yet
- Android app (the web app works in Android Chrome), end-to-end encrypted photos, and an outside security review.
