# Accessibility sweep, Nov 1, 2026

Chase asked for "an accessibility sweep". This one ran on the web demo build (sample data, no live data) after the warm palette and Atkinson font (PR #88) merged.

**Result:** Hearth passes the big checks. No text under 17 pt, every control has a name, every tap target is 48 pt or more, no sideways scroll at 320 px, and every color pair passes WCAG AA in light and dark. Three problems were found and fixed in this PR, and six smaller ones are listed for later.

## How it was checked
- `scripts/ux-rules.mjs` and `scripts/ux-shots.mjs` (studio tools) on the Photos screen at phone, laptop and 320 px widths, light and dark.
- A wider pass with Playwright on 10 views in light and dark: Photos, the full-screen viewer, a post's More menu, Comments, Messages, By date, Share, Groups, Me and the demo drawer, plus Grandma Jo's Photos and Help. It measured text size, text contrast against the real background, tap target size, control names, heading levels, image alt text and sideways scroll.
- Every palette pair computed by hand (table in `docs/product/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, "Palette").
- **Not checked:** VoiceOver or TalkBack on a real phone, text at 200% on a phone, Android. Those need a phone (milestone 1 UAT).

Screenshots: `docs/design/a11y-2026-11-01/`.

## Fixed in this PR (test-first, `app/src/ui/__tests__/a11ySweep.test.tsx`)

| ID | Problem | Rule | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A11Y-1 | Every heading was level 1: Photos had 3, Me had 14. A screen reader's heading list was a flat pile with no outline | WCAG 1.3.1, G9 | The screen title is the only level-1 heading; card and section titles are level 2 |
| A11Y-2 | On the web, a switch turned On showed a teal thumb on a blue track (the browser default) | V6 color restraint | The thumb uses Hearth's card color |
| A11Y-3 | A switch turned Off had a track at 1.5:1 against the card, so it nearly vanished in light mode | WCAG 1.4.11 (3:1 for controls) | The Off track uses the muted color (7:1 light, 7.7:1 dark) |
| DS-13 | 9 different corner sizes and 13 different gaps across the app | V3, V5 | Spacing and corner tokens in `theme.ts`; the shared kit now uses only them |

## Open (not built; for grandparent-ux and the TRACKER)

| ID | Finding | Rule | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A11Y-4 | The full-screen photo sits on the light screen color. Every photo app people know (Photos, Facebook, Instagram, Google Photos) shows a full-screen photo on black | Familiar patterns (P-4) | Black behind the full-screen photo in both looks, with the words and buttons on a solid bar (DS-6) |
| A11Y-5 | The full-screen viewer has no heading | G9 | Make "From Kai · Fri, Oct 30" the level-1 heading |
| A11Y-6 | "◀ Back" and "Next ▶" use text arrows, not the phone's own icons | V9 | Use `chevron.left` / `chevron.right` (SF Symbols) and `chevron_left` / `chevron_right` (Material) |
| A11Y-7 | On Me and Help, the Bigger text and Light or dark choices are bordered boxes inside a card | V9 card in a card | Drop the outer card border, or make each choice a plain row with a divider (iPhone grouped list) |
| A11Y-8 | Screens still pick their own corners and gaps (PostCard 4/20/22, events 20, gaps of 6, 10, 14, 18) | DS-13 | Move to `SPACE` and `RADIUS` as each screen is touched (TRACKER R30) |
| A11Y-9 | The reaction row is icons only, with no words under them | DS-3 | Chase's call (REACT-7, Oct 25) and each has a spoken label, so it stays. Recheck when a relative uses it |

## False alarms (checked and cleared)
- The reaction emoji measured 1.3:1 in dark mode, but emoji are drawn in their own colors, so text contrast doesn't apply.
- The web switch's hidden checkbox is 40×20 with no name, but the whole row is the control, is 56 pt tall and has the name.
- A 16 px glyph is the Details chevron icon, not text.
- `ux-rules` reported 0 level-1 headings on the first pass because it measured before the demo finished loading; there were 3.
