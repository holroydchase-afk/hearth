# Decision: compact Photos toolbar, Hearth's tab bar on iPhone, quieter reaction and who-can-see
Date: Nov 5, 2026 · Status: Chase asked (live review on his iPhone: "Messages should be a single icon so should filters both of those are extremely common"; "Yes" to the two look fixes) · DEC-52

What changes:
- **LIVE-1:** the iPhone's glass tab bar drew our 17 pt words over the icons, so iPhone uses Hearth's own tab bar (the web one). Android keeps its own bar.
- **LIVE-2:** Messages, By date and Events become compact icon buttons in a toolbar above the big title, a 17 pt word under each icon (not icon-only: DS-3, rule 3).
- **LIVE-3:** my reaction is a soft fill, without the heavy outline.
- **LIVE-4:** the who-can-see circles get the word "Can see" in front.
- Over: icon-only buttons (a grandparent can't tell a sort icon from a menu); 10 pt tab words (DS-2); keeping the half-width pills.
