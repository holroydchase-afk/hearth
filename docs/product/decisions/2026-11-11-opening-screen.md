# Decision: Hearth opens on "New for you"
Date: Nov 11, 2026 · Status: Chase picked C ("Go with C and all your recommendations") · DEC-58

What changes (`docs/product/SPEC-OPENING.md`, OPEN-1..11):
- Photos opens with a New for you card when photos came in since your last visit: a collage of them, "New since Tuesday, from Mom and Dad", one button **See the new photos**.
- That button walks the new posts one at a time with Back and Next (the picture-frame option, D).
- A **Wall** button on Photos opens the photo wall (the gallery option, A); every tile opens the photo and its spoken label names the sender.
- The posts list (B) stays underneath.
- Over: the photo wall as the front door (prettiest, but tiles hide who sent a photo and who can see it, and faces are small for the oldest relatives); one photo at a time as the front door (slow for parents); leaving Photos as is (no clear "this is new").

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PA Posting parent | Green | Their own posts never show as new; the wall gives the gallery view |
| FA Far-away inviter | Green | One button to tell a relative about: "tap See the new photos" |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Green | One big button, then one post at a time with Back and Next |
| AG Android grandpa | Green | Same buttons on Android; no gestures needed |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Green | Only posts they can already see; no group names |
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | Same screen on the web view |
| CU Cousin | Green | The wall looks like the photo apps they know |
| TR Tech reader | Green | "Last visit" stays on the phone; nothing new is stored or sent |
