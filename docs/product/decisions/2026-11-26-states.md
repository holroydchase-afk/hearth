# Decision: Every list screen says what happened, keeps "Your photos are safe", and gives one button
Date: Nov 26, 2026 · Status: specced (staff-pm), defaults taken while Chase is away: Help number shows the inviter's name always and the number only when confirmed as theirs; empty viewer Gallery gets "Check again"; help email stays "Decide later" · `docs/product/SPEC-STATES.md`, STATES-1..24

What changes: a lost signal shows a plain line, "Try again" and "Your photos are safe" that stay until it works; empty lists say what to do next with a verb button; Help opens on who to call.

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | Never opens the app; her email and the web page are untouched |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Red | "Something went wrong" is vague; "Check again" must visibly do something |
| AG Android grandpa | Red | Spotty signal is his daily case; Try again must be 48 dp and not clip |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Green | Empty words never name a household; Help names whoever invited her |
| CU Cousin | Green | Plain states cost her nothing; "Make a group" is one tap |
| PA Posting parent | Green | Share photos button on empty Gallery; no new steps |
| FA Far-away inviter | Red | Help number is one build-wide setting; wrong number sends relatives to the wrong person |
| TR Tech reader | Green | No API, storage or logging change; error text never logged |

Resolution: LG mitigation: next line says "Your photos are safe. Tap Try again" and "Check again" shows "Checking…" then "Still nothing yet" (STATES-1, STATES-24); confirm in STATES-M4 with a real grandparent.
Resolution: AG mitigation: Try again 48 dp at 360 wide, Easy view On and Off, checked on a real Android phone (STATES-M2).
Resolution: FA mitigation: name always shows, number only when marked as the inviter's, no contact invented (STATES-20, STATES-23); help email stays "Decide later" and the row stays hidden until set (STATES-21).
