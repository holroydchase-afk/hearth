# Decision: a web app for Android Chrome first; Hearth runs on web, iPhone and Android
Date: Nov 24, 2026 · Status: decided (Chase, "go") · Spec: `docs/product/SPEC-WEB-APP.md` · TRACKER R92, R93 · DEC-88

What changes: the two Android beta grandparents use Hearth in Chrome (home-screen icon, stays signed in) before a native Android build. Order: staying signed in, add to home screen, photo sharing, email notices, Save to my phone as a download. $0, no domain.
Over: waiting for a native Android build (ANCHORS milestone 5), or leaving the Android grandpas out of the beta.
