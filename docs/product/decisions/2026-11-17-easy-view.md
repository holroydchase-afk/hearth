# Decision: Easy view, on for grandparents, off for parents
Date: Nov 17, 2026 · Status: Chase's ("labels under tab icons should be off by default", a profile setting "Easy view"; "On for grandparents, off for parents", Nov 17) · DEC-66 · Spec: `docs/product/SPEC-REDESIGN.md` (RD-74..RD-81)

What changes: one setting in More, "Easy view" (On/Off in words), replaces Bigger text and adds words under the tab icons. Viewers start On; owners and adults start Off and see icon-only tabs that still speak their names. A saved Bigger text choice carries over.
Over: words under every tab for everyone; a separate tab-words switch; Off for everyone.

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | Frame and email unchanged |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Green | Viewer, so words and bigger text are on with nothing to set |
| AG Android grandpa | Green | Same default on Android; tab words fit at 360 pt (RD-81) |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Green | Viewer default On; nothing about who sees what changes |
| CU Cousin | Green | Icon-only tabs if she posts; one switch if she wants words |
| PA Posting parent | Green | Cleaner tab bar like the apps parents already use |
| FA Far-away inviter | Red | Their tab bar has no words while Grandma's has them |
| TR Tech reader | Green | A choice saved on the phone only; no data leaves it |

Resolution: FA mitigation: same tabs, same order and same icons on both phones, and the words are the ones FA hears from VoiceOver; Grandma's screen is the one with words, which is the one being read aloud. FA can turn Easy view on in one step (RD-78) and the demo's "See it as Grandma Jo" shows it.
