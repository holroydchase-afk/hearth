# Hearth people: example family (composites)

Hearth is built for a real family first (milestones in `docs/product/ANCHORS.md`, story in `docs/product/STORY.md`), but this repo is public. So the people below are **fictional composites**: they keep the shape of the family (who needs what from Hearth) and none of the private details. Names are the generic nicknames kids use for grandparents. Who is related to whom, who is separated from whom, which phone each person has and where anyone lives stay out of the repo. The real key lives in the project's private files.

Tech-savvy ranking: the ranked persona cards live in `docs/product/PERSONA-CHECK.md`.

## What the family needs (from the story)

- **Several grandparent households on each side.** Some are separate households that would rather not share a conversation. "People who won't sit in the same room certainly won't comment on each other's photos." A post may go to more than one household; what one household writes must not reach another unless that's how the groups are set up.
- **Android and iPhone alike.** Everyone gets the same full-quality photo (and later video), whichever phone they have.
- **Stepparents are full grandparents.** They should never feel second-tier (A2): no rankings, no "seen by" that singles anyone out.
- **New partners.** A grandparent may find a new partner. They may join later, or may be kept out; groups have to handle both without drama.
- **Great-grandparents.** They may never install an app. A smart frame is one way in; email is another.
- **The parents' guilt.** The parents want to share often without spamming, and without worrying who sees what (A1).
